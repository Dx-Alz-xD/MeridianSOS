MeridianSOS

Coupled flood and road-hazard forecasting for Meridian City — a synthetic 4.2 million-person Atlantic coastal metropolis. A physics simulator generates the ground truth; a machine-learning surrogate reproduces it fast enough to warn people.

The live side: zones, gauges, shelters and alerts seeded from the city model, with check-ins, presence and messaging for a field response team.
Meridian City is synthetic. Its parameters are calibrated against a real North African Atlantic-coast metro so the hydrology behaves plausibly. The method transfers to a real city; the weights do not.
